About me

Building reliable and trustworthy multimodal AI systems.

I am a PhD candidate in Computing and Information Systems at the University of Melbourne, supervised by Prof. Christopher Leckie, Prof. Sarah M. Erfani, and Dr. Hanxun Huang. My research focuses on trustworthy AI, particularly robustness, fairness, reliability, and hallucination in vision-language models.

My work studies how multimodal systems fail under distribution shifts, spurious correlations, adversarial perturbations, and visually unsupported generation. I develop practical methods grounded in representation geometry, local density, and internal model evidence.

I am happy to connect about research collaborations, internships, and applied research opportunities.
